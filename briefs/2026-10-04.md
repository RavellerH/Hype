# Hyperliquid (HYPE) Price Rises by 1.97% Amid Moderate Positive Funding and Substantial Open Interest

*2026-10-04 · confidence: medium*

HYPE demonstrated a positive 24-hour performance, with its price increasing by 1.97% to $89.79. HYPE's funding rate is on par with ETH and SUI, and marginally higher than BTC's +0.0011% and SOL's +0.0007%, reflecting a relatively strong, albeit not overheated, bullish sentiment for the asset compared to some majors.
