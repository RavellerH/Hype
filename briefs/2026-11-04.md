# Hyperliquid Sees Stagnant HYPE Price Amidst Uniformly Positive Funding and High Open Interest

*2026-11-04 · confidence: medium*

## Market Analysis
HYPE's price exhibited marginal movement, recording a -0.11% decrease over 24 hours to 83.49. Despite this near-flat price action, the 8-hour funding rate for HYPE stood at a positive +0.0013%, mirroring identical positive rates across ETH, SOL, and SUI, and slightly above BTC's +0.0012%. This widespread positive funding indicates a prevailing slight demand for long positions across the platform's major perpetuals, including HYPE, even without significant upward price momentum. HYPE's open interest (OI) is notably high at 1.85B, positioning it as the third-largest by OI on the platform, trailing only BTC (3.07B) and ETH (2.29B), and significantly surpassing SOL (521.7M). The Hyperliquid platform recorded a robust 24-hour total perp volume of 6.43B, indicating active trading across the listed perpetuals. The juxtaposition of a flat HYPE price with sustained positive funding and high OI suggests that while long positions are being maintained or opened, they are not currently driving significant price discovery, potentially indicating a state of equilibrium or absorption of buying pressure.

## Risks
- The prevailing 'Fear' sentiment, as indicated by a Fear & Greed Index score of 34, presents a potential broader market headwind that could undermine the current positive funding rates across Hyperliquid assets.
- Despite positive funding, HYPE's price stagnation (-0.11%) suggests long positions are not translating into upward price movement, increasing the risk of deleveraging if market sentiment shifts or if long positions are liquidated without accompanying spot price support.
- The significant open interest in HYPE (1.85B) represents a concentrated risk, as a rapid shift in sentiment or platform-specific event could trigger substantial unwinding of positions, exacerbating price volatility.

## Opportunities
- The consistent positive 8-hour funding rates across BTC, ETH, SOL, HYPE, and SUI suggest a persistent, albeit slight, demand for long exposure on the platform, which could support prices if broader market conditions improve.
- The HyperEVM TVL of 2.53B demonstrates robust ecosystem engagement and liquidity, potentially serving as a fundamental driver for HYPE token value over time, distinct from immediate trading dynamics.
- HYPE's substantial open interest of 1.85B, making it the third most active perp by OI on the platform, indicates significant trader interest and potential for amplified price movements should a clear trend emerge.

## News
