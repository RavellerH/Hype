All identified setups (BTC, ETH, SUI) exhibit 'SPOT DRIVEN' money flow. This crucial signal means that price increases are accompanied by rising Cumulative Volume Delta (CVD) and either flat or declining Open Interest (OI). Such a pattern indicates genuine demand from spot buyers, with existing short positions likely covering, rather than price action being fueled by new leveraged long entries or a short squeeze. This type of flow is generally considered healthier and more sustainable for a bullish trend.
