05 / 08

Trading philosophy

Built around
how I trade.

01
Follow the market. Don't fight it.
Every alert is trend-following, not counter-trend. When all timeframes align, the system tells you. When they don't, it stays quiet.
02
Watch the flow in and out.
Stablecoin supply, exchange reserves, OI changes, liquidation clusters. Money flow tells the story before price does.
03
Read the chart before the news.
TA signals fire first. News analysis adds context — confirms or questions what the chart already said.
04
On-chain is context, not noise.
Regime signal (ACCUMULATION / DISTRIBUTION / WATCH) built from network health, funding, stablecoins, and liquidation data.
