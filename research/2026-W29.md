## Summary
This past week, HYPE experienced a significant price correction, dropping sharply mid-week before stabilizing with minor fluctuations, resulting in a net decline. Funding rates initially showed a strong HYPE-specific long bias but quickly shifted to a uniformly low positive rate across major assets, signaling a loss of asset-specific directional conviction.
